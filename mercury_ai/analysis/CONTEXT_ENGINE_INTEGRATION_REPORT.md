# CONTEXT_ENGINE_INTEGRATION_REPORT.md

## Responsibilities
The `ContextEngine` acts as the central aggregator, responsible for merging, deduplicating, and analyzing all evidence generated by the analysis engines (Trend, Momentum, VWAP, Liquidity, Smart Money, Price Action, Volume, Session, Calendar, News, Support Resistance).

## Contract
- **Input**: `List[Evidence]`, `MarketData` (or similar market state)
- **Output**: `MarketContext` (Immutable, containing `MarketEvidenceBundle`)

## Validation
- Merges evidence streams.
- Performs deduplication based on source engine and evidence type.
- Detects conflicts between evidence points (e.g., conflicting trend direction).
- Assesses overall context quality metrics.

## Integration
- Integrates with `PipelineExecutor` to ensure standardized execution, profiling, and audit trail generation.
- Aggregates outputs into the unified `MarketContext` model.
